The agenda gathers the TODO items, dates and tags of your org files into
one list you can act on: change a state, reschedule, search and filter,
and do the same to many entries at once. It builds on the agenda part of
the second lesson (=:Org tutor workflow=).

- As before, a {{done}} at the end of an exercise's headline means it is
  done, and {{todo}} that it is not yet. Keys are shown as mapped in your
  config; =<leader>= is ={{leader}}=.
- This is a working copy: =:w= saves your progress and =:Org tutor
  agenda reset= starts over. In the menu, =<= restricts the agenda to
  this file, so your notes stay out of it.
- Stuck? =:Org tutor_hint= shows the keys that solve the exercise at
  the cursor, and =:Org tutor= lists every lesson.

* Lesson 1: the dispatcher
={{global.agenda}}= opens the agenda menu, the dispatcher. Each key in it
is a view: =a= the week, =t= every TODO item, =m= a tags search, =s= a
word search. =<= first restricts the view to this file (twice: to the
subtree at the cursor). In a view, ={{agenda.quit}}= closes it and ={{agenda.help}}= lists its keys.

** 1.1 The TODO list
Press ={{global.agenda}}=, =<= and =t=: every TODO item of this file. On
=Renew the passport= press ={{agenda.priority_up}}=: it gets priority B, in the file
too. Press it again for A; ={{agenda.priority_down}}= lowers it.

*** TODO Renew the passport

** 1.2 The week
Press ={{global.agenda}}=, =<=, =a=. =Pay the rent= is overdue, so it
shows on today. ={{agenda.later}}= and ={{agenda.earlier}}= go a week forward and back, ={{agenda.today}}= comes
back to today, and ={{agenda.day_view}}= / ={{agenda.week_view}}= show one day or the week. On
=Pay the rent= press ={{agenda.todo}}=: it is DONE.

* Lesson 2: dates from the agenda
The agenda changes the entries themselves: every key below edits the
headline in its file, wherever that file is.

** 2.1 Reschedule
=Call the bank= was scheduled a week ago. Open the week
(={{global.agenda}}=, =<=, =a=) and on it press ={{agenda.schedule}}=:
the calendar opens on its date. =.= goes to today and =<CR>= picks it.
={{agenda.date_later}}= moves a date one day later, without a prompt.

*** TODO Call the bank
SCHEDULED: <2026-10-05 Mon>

* Lesson 3: searching
=m= matches tags (and properties): =home= is every entry tagged
=:home:=, =work+errand= both tags, =work-errand= work but not errand.
=s= searches words in the headlines and their text.

** 3.1 Match a tag
Press ={{global.agenda}}=, =<=, =m=, type =errand= and =<CR>=. On
=Buy stamps= press ={{agenda.todo}}= to mark it DONE.

*** TODO Buy stamps                                             :errand:
*** TODO Water the garden                                         :home:

** 3.2 Search words
Press ={{global.agenda}}=, =<=, =s=, type =invoice= and =<CR>=: the
entry that mentions it in its text. ={{agenda.switch_to}}= on it opens it in place of the
agenda (={{agenda.goto}}= opens it in another window).

* Lesson 4: many entries at once
={{agenda.mark}}= marks the line at the cursor (={{agenda.unmark}}= unmarks it), ={{agenda.mark_all}}= marks every
line shown, and ={{agenda.bulk_action}}= acts on all the marked entries: change their
state, add a tag, schedule, set a deadline, archive or refile them.

** 4.1 Mark and bulk schedule
In the TODO list (={{global.agenda}}=, =<=, =t=), press ={{agenda.mark}}= on
=Tidy the shed= and on =Fix the bike=, then ={{agenda.bulk_action}}= and =s=, type
=+1d= and =<CR>=: both are scheduled for tomorrow. (=<CR>= alone opens
the calendar.)

*** TODO Tidy the shed                                              :home:
*** TODO Fix the bike                                               :home:

** 4.2 Filter, then act on what is left
In the TODO list, press ={{agenda.filter}}=, type =+work= and =<CR>=: only the
=:work:= entries are left (=-work= hides them instead, and ={{agenda.filter_remove}}=
removes the filter). Then ={{agenda.mark_all}}=, ={{agenda.bulk_action}}= and =d=, type =fri= and
=<CR>=: each gets a deadline on Friday.

*** TODO Send the report                                            :work:
*** TODO Book the train                                             :work:

* Lesson 5: your own views
=n= in the dispatcher is a block agenda: the week, then every TODO item,
in one buffer. Your own views go in =agenda.custom_commands= in
=setup()=, under a key of the menu, such as this one on =h=:

#+begin_src lua
agenda = {
  custom_commands = {
    h = { description = "Home", types = {
      { type = "agenda", span = "day" },
      { type = "tags_todo", match = "home" },
    } },
  },
},
#+end_src

** 5.1 A block agenda
Press ={{global.agenda}}=, =<=, =n=. ={{agenda.next_item}}= and ={{agenda.prev_item}}= move from entry to
entry. On =Read the manual=, in the TODO block, press ={{agenda.todo}}=.
