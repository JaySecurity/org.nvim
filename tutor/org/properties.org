Properties are named values on a headline, such as a colour, an owner
or how long a task will take. Column view shows them as a table over
your outline, where you can edit them and see their sums.

- As before, a {{done}} at the end of an exercise's headline means it is
  done, and {{todo}} that it is not yet. Keys are shown as mapped in your
  config; =<leader>= is ={{leader}}=.
- This is a working copy: =:w= saves your progress and =:Org tutor
  properties reset= starts over. Nothing here touches your notes.
- Stuck? =:Org tutor_hint= shows the keys that solve the exercise at
  the cursor, and =:Org tutor= lists every lesson.

* Lesson 1: properties
Properties live in a =:PROPERTIES:= drawer right under the headline, one
=:Name: value= per line. You rarely type the drawer yourself: the keys
below make it for you. Drawers start folded; ={{org.cycle}}= on the
=:PROPERTIES:= line opens and closes one.

** 1.1 Set a property
Put the cursor on =Coffee grinder= and press ={{org.set_property}}=. Type
the name =Color= and =<CR>=, then the value =red= and =<CR>=. A drawer
with =:Color: red= appears under the headline.

*** Coffee grinder

** 1.2 Cycle through allowed values
A =:Size_ALL:= property lists the values =Size= may take, in its whole
subtree (an =_ALL= property is inherited). Open the drawer of =Blue
shirt= with ={{org.cycle}}= on its =:PROPERTIES:= line. Then on =:Size: M=
press ={{org.shift_right}}=: it changes to the next allowed value, =L=
(={{org.shift_left}}= goes back).

*** Shirts
:PROPERTIES:
:Size_ALL: S M L XL
:END:
**** Blue shirt
:PROPERTIES:
:Size:     M
:END:

** 1.3 Pick an allowed value
When a property has allowed values, ={{org.set_property}}= offers them
in a menu. On =Red shirt=, set =Size= to =S=: press the key, type =Size=
and =<CR>=, then pick =S=.

*** Shirts
:PROPERTIES:
:Size_ALL: S M L XL
:END:
**** Red shirt

** 1.4 Set an effort
=Effort= is the property for how long a task should take, as =H:MM=.
The clock, the agenda and column view use it. On =Write the intro=,
press ={{org.set_effort}}=, type =0:45= and =<CR>=.

*** TODO Write the intro

** 1.5 Delete a property
On =Old lamp=, press ={{org.delete_property}}= and pick =Color=. The
drawer goes too, as nothing is left in it.

*** Old lamp
:PROPERTIES:
:Color:    green
:END:

* Lesson 2: column view
Column view draws the properties of a subtree as columns over its
headlines. A =#+COLUMNS:= line sets the columns for the whole file:

: #+COLUMNS: %25ITEM %Effort{:} %Owner

Each =%Name= is a column and =ITEM= is the headline. A number before the
name is the column's width, and ={:}= after it adds up the times of the
children in their parent. A =:COLUMNS:= property sets the columns for
one subtree, as in the trees below. Most properties belong to their own
headline only: =COLUMNS= and the =_ALL= ones are among the few that
are inherited.

** 2.1 Open column view
On =Website= press ={{org.column_view}}=. Its headlines become rows,
and =Website= shows the sum of the efforts below it, =3:30=. Press =q=
to close the view again.

*** Website
:PROPERTIES:
:COLUMNS:  %ITEM %Effort{:} %Owner
:END:
**** Design the pages
:PROPERTIES:
:Effort:   2:00
:END:
**** Write the copy
:PROPERTIES:
:Effort:   1:30
:END:

** 2.2 Cycle a value in column view
Open column view on =Garden=. =l= and =h= move across the columns, =j=
and =k= across the rows. On the =Owner= column of =Dig the beds=, press
=n= until it shows =Ben= (=p= goes back). Then =q= closes the view.

*** Garden
:PROPERTIES:
:COLUMNS:  %ITEM %Effort{:} %Owner
:Owner_ALL: Ana Ben Cleo
:END:
**** Dig the beds
:PROPERTIES:
:Owner:    Ana
:END:

** 2.3 Edit a value and watch the sum
Open column view on =Kitchen=. On the =Effort= column of =Paint the
walls=, press =e=, type =3:00= and =<CR>=: the sum on =Kitchen= goes
from =0:30= to =3:30=. Press =q= when you are done.

*** Kitchen
:PROPERTIES:
:COLUMNS:  %ITEM %Effort{:}
:END:
**** Paint the walls
**** Fix the tap
:PROPERTIES:
:Effort:   0:30
:END:

** 2.4 Insert a columnview block
A columnview block writes the column view of a subtree into the file,
as a table. Put the cursor on the =Report:= line below and press
={{emacs.insert_columnview}}=, then =<CR>= at the prompt (=local= is
the subtree the block is in).

*** Shop
:PROPERTIES:
:COLUMNS:  %ITEM %Effort{:}
:END:
Report:
**** Order paint
:PROPERTIES:
:Effort:   0:20
:END:

** 2.5 Update a columnview block
=Pack the boxes= now takes =2:00=, but the table below still says
=1:00=. Put the cursor on its =#+BEGIN:= line and press
={{org.context_action}}= (or ={{org.dblock_update}}=): the table is
written again, with the new times.

*** Move
:PROPERTIES:
:COLUMNS:  %ITEM %Effort{:}
:END:
#+BEGIN: columnview :hlines 1 :id local
| ITEM           | Effort |
|----------------+--------|
| Move           |   1:00 |
| Pack the boxes |   1:00 |
#+END:
**** Pack the boxes
:PROPERTIES:
:Effort:   2:00
:END:
