This lesson is about dates: typing them quickly at the date prompt,
changing them with a key, tasks that repeat, deadlines that warn you
early, and ranges. It assumes the first lesson (=:Org tutor basics=).

* Lesson 1: the date prompt
A date in org is a timestamp such as =<2026-10-12 Mon>=. Every key that
asks for a date opens the same prompt: a calendar where =h= =l= move a
day, =j= =k= a week, =.= goes to today and =<CR>= picks the date. Press
=i= there to type the date instead.

** 1.1 Insert today's date
Put the cursor at the end of =Dentist:= below, press ={{org.timestamp}}=
and then =<CR>=: today's date goes in at the cursor.

** 1.2 Type a relative date
Put the cursor on =Call the plumber=, press ={{org.schedule}}=, then =i=,
type =+2d= and =<CR>=. Two days from today. =+1w= is a week, =+1m= a
month, =-1d= yesterday; =.= or =+0= is today.

* Lesson 2: changing a date
No need to retype a date to change it: put the cursor on it and use
Shift with the arrow keys.

** 2.1 One day later
Put the cursor on the date below and press ={{org.shift_right}}= twice:
the day moves forward, and the weekday follows. ={{org.shift_left}}=
goes back.

- Party: <2026-10-12 Mon>

** 2.2 Change one part
={{org.shift_up}}= and ={{org.shift_down}}= change the part under the
cursor: the year, month, day, hour or minutes (by 5). Put the cursor
on the hour =10= below and press ={{org.shift_up}}=.

- Call: <2026-10-12 Mon 10:00>

* Lesson 3: repeating tasks
A repeater after the date makes a task come back: =+1w= every week,
=.+1d= one day after you last did it, =++1m= every month, always to a
date in the future. Marking such a task DONE moves its date on instead.

** 3.1 Add a repeater
Put the cursor on the =>= of the date under =Water the plants=, press
=i=, type a space and =+1w=, then =<Esc>=. The task now repeats every
week.
