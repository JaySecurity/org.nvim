Notes pile up. This lesson shows how to keep a file in order: move
entries where they belong (refile), put finished work out of the way
(archive), and find things again: sparse trees, jumping to a heading,
narrowing to one subtree and searching tags in the agenda.

- As before, a {{done}} at the end of an exercise's headline means it is
  done, and {{todo}} that it is not yet. Keys are shown as mapped in your
  config; =<leader>= is ={{leader}}=.
- This is a working copy: =:w= saves your progress and =:Org tutor
  organize reset= starts over. The =#+ARCHIVE:= line at the top sends
  archived entries to a file next to this copy, away from your notes.
- Stuck? =:Org tutor_hint= shows the keys that solve the exercise at
  the cursor, and =:Org tutor= lists every lesson.

* Lesson 1: refile
Refiling moves a subtree, with everything in it, under another
headline. The targets are the top-level headlines of the file, unless
the =refile.targets= option names other files and levels.

** 1.1 Refile a task
Put the cursor on =Buy stamps= and press ={{org.refile}}=. Pick =Errands=
in the list: the task moves to the end of =Errands=, the headline after
this lesson.

*** TODO Book the flights

** 1.2 Copy instead of moving
={{org.refile_copy}}= files a copy and keeps the original. On =Renew the
passport= press ={{org.refile_copy}}= and pick =Errands=: the task is
now in both places.

*** TODO Renew the passport

* Lesson 2: archive
Archiving puts done work out of the way, so the outline and the agenda
stay short. Nothing is lost: archived entries keep their text.

** 2.1 Tag a subtree ARCHIVE
On =Old ideas= press ={{org.toggle_archive_tag}}=. The ARCHIVE tag keeps
a subtree folded (={{org.cycle}}= leaves it closed) and out of the
agenda, while it stays where it is. The same key removes the tag.

*** Old ideas
- A bigger desk
- A tea kettle

** 2.2 Archive under the Archive sibling
On =Fix the fence= press ={{emacs.archive_to_sibling}}=: it moves under an
=Archive= headline next to it, which is made, with the ARCHIVE tag, the
first time.

*** DONE Fix the fence
*** TODO Paint the shed

** 2.3 Archive to a file
On =Pay the rent= press ={{org.archive_subtree}}=: the subtree leaves this
file for =organize.org_archive=, next to it. The =#+ARCHIVE:= line or
an =ARCHIVE= property says where; =archive_location= is the default.

*** DONE Pay the rent

* Lesson 3: sparse trees
A sparse tree folds the whole file down to the entries that match, with
the headlines above them, and highlights the matches.
={{org.sparse_tree}}= shows the menu of searches.

** 3.1 Show the TODO entries
Press ={{org.sparse_tree}}= and then =t=: only the open TODO entries of
the file show. =Water the garden= is DONE, so it folds away.

*** TODO Call the plumber
*** DONE Water the garden
The roses too.

** 3.2 Search for a word
Press ={{org.sparse_tree}}=, =r=, type =invoice= and press =<CR>=: the
entries with the word in their text open, the word highlighted.

*** Shop
Send the invoice to Anna.
*** Garden
Nothing to send here.

** 3.3 Match a tag
Press ={{org.sparse_tree}}=, =m=, type =phone= and press =<CR>=: the
headlines tagged =phone= show. A match can combine tags: =phone-work=.

*** Call the bank                                                   :phone:
Ask about the card.
*** Read the contract                                                :work:
Before Friday.

** 3.4 Back to the whole file
Press ={{org.sparse_tree}}= and then =C= to clear the highlights, and
={{org.global_cycle}}= until the whole file shows again.

* Lesson 4: jump and focus
In a long file, go straight to a heading, work on one subtree with
nothing else in view, or let the agenda find entries by their tags.

** 4.1 Jump to a heading
={{org.buffer_goto}}= shows the outline of this file in a tab of its own.
Type =What next= to search for it, press =<CR>= to end the search and
=<CR>= again to jump there. The folds of this file stay as they were.

** 4.2 Narrow to a subtree
On =Packing list= press ={{org.narrow_subtree}}=: the subtree opens alone
in an edit buffer. Add an item =- Sunscreen= at the end, then press
={{edit_src.save_exit}}= to write it back and close it.

*** Packing list
- Passport
- Charger

** 4.3 Search tags in the agenda
The agenda searches tags across files. Press ={{global.agenda}}=, then
=<= (this file only), =m=, type =errand= and press =<CR>=. On =Return
the books= press ={{agenda.goto}}= to jump to it.

*** TODO Pick up the parcel                                       :errand:
