An org table is more than lined-up text: you move around it like a
form, reshape it with a key, and give it formulas that compute
columns and totals, like a small spreadsheet. The first lesson showed
how to align a table and add a row; this one goes further.

- As before, a {{done}} at the end of an exercise's headline means it is
  done, and {{todo}} that it is not yet. Keys are shown as mapped in your
  config; =<leader>= is ={{leader}}=.
- This is a working copy: =:w= saves your progress and =:Org tutor
  tables reset= starts over.
- Stuck? =:Org tutor_hint= shows the keys that solve the exercise at
  the cursor, and =:Org tutor= lists every lesson.

* Lesson 1: moving around
In Insert mode a table works like a form. Every move lines the table
up again, and the first character you type after a move replaces the
field's text.

** 1.1 Next and previous field
={{org_insert.insert_tab}}= goes to the next field,
={{org_insert.table_prev_field}}= to the previous one. On =Milk= press
=i=, then ={{org_insert.insert_tab}}= twice to reach its price, type
=1.20= and press =<Esc>=.

| Item  | Qty | Price |
|-------+-----+-------|
| Bread |   1 |  2.50 |
| Milk  |   2 |       |

** 1.2 Down a column
=<CR>= goes to the same field of the next row. On =Eggs= press =i= and
={{org_insert.insert_tab}}=, type =12=, press =<CR>=, type =3= and press
=<Esc>=.

| Item  | Qty |
|-------+-----|
| Eggs  |     |
| Flour |     |

* Lesson 2: reshaping
In Normal mode the keys that move headlines move table columns and
rows, and their Shift versions delete and insert them.

** 2.1 Move a column
={{org.meta_left}}= and ={{org.meta_right}}= move the column at the
cursor. Put the =Qty= column first: on =Qty= press ={{org.meta_left}}=.

| Item   | Qty |
|--------+-----|
| Apples |   4 |
| Pears  |   2 |

** 2.2 Move a row
={{org.meta_up}}= and ={{org.meta_down}}= move the row. Put =Coffee= at
the top: on its row press ={{org.meta_up}}=.

| Drink  | Cups |
|--------+------|
| Tea    |    2 |
| Coffee |    3 |

** 2.3 Delete a column
={{org.shift_meta_left}}= deletes the column at the cursor,
={{org.shift_meta_right}}= inserts an empty one. Delete the =Notes=
column: on =Notes= press ={{org.shift_meta_left}}=.

| Item  | Notes | Qty |
|-------+-------+-----|
| Rice  | dry   |   1 |
| Beans | dry   |   2 |

** 2.4 Delete a row
={{org.shift_meta_up}}= deletes the row at the cursor,
={{org.shift_meta_down}}= inserts an empty one above it. Delete the
=Old= row: on =Old= press ={{org.shift_meta_up}}=.

| Task  | Hours |
|-------+-------|
| Write |     3 |
| Old   |     9 |
| Read  |     1 |

** 2.5 Add a separator line
={{org.table_insert_hline}}= puts a line under the current row. Separate
the header: on =Day= press ={{org.table_insert_hline}}=.

| Day | Steps |
| Mon |  4200 |
| Tue |  6100 |

** 2.6 Sort the rows
={{org.table_sort}}= sorts the rows by the column at the cursor. It asks
how: =a= alphabetically, =n= by number (=A= and =N= in reverse). On a
number of =Score= press ={{org.table_sort}}= and =N=.

| Name | Score |
|------+-------|
| Ana  |    12 |
| Ben  |    31 |
| Cleo |    25 |

* Lesson 3: formulas
Formulas live in a =#+TBLFM:= line under the table. =$2= is column 2 of
the same row, =@3= row 3 (separator lines don't count), =@>= the last
row and =@-1= the row above.

** 3.1 A column formula
Text typed in a field that starts with an equals sign becomes the
formula of its column. On =Apples= press =i= and
={{org_insert.insert_tab}}= three times, type ~=$2*$3~ (Qty times
Price) and press ={{org_insert.insert_tab}}=. A =#+TBLFM:= line appears.

| Item   | Qty | Price | Total |
|--------+-----+-------+-------|
| Apples |   3 |   0.5 |       |
| Lemons |   4 |  0.25 |       |

** 3.2 Ask for a formula
={{emacs.table_formula}}= asks for the formula of the column at the
cursor and computes that one field. On the =0= next to =10= press
={{emacs.table_formula}}=, type =$1*0.62= (km to miles) and press =<CR>=.

| Km | Miles |
|----+-------|
| 10 |     0 |
| 42 |     0 |

** 3.3 Recalculate
A new formula fills only the field you typed it in. To compute every
field again, press ={{org.table_recalc}}= in the table. On a =#+TBLFM:=
line ={{org.context_action}}= does the same.

| Item   | Qty | Price | Total |
|--------+-----+-------+-------|
| Apples |   3 |   0.5 |       |
| Lemons |   4 |  0.25 |       |
#+TBLFM: $4=$2*$3

** 3.4 A field formula
Starting with ~:=~ instead makes a formula for that one field. On =Sum=
press =i= and ={{org_insert.insert_tab}}=, type ~:=vsum(@2..@-1)~, then
=<Esc>= and ={{org.context_action}}=: it sums row 2 to the row above.

| Item | Cost |
|------+------|
| Rent |  800 |
| Food |  250 |
|------+------|
| Sum  |      |

** 3.5 A number format
A format after =;= shapes a formula's results: =%.2f= shows two
decimals, so =1.= becomes =1.00=. Press =A= on the =#+TBLFM:= line, type
=;%.2f=, then =<Esc>= and ={{org.context_action}}= to recalculate.

| Item   | Qty | Price | Total |
|--------+-----+-------+-------|
| Apples |   3 |   0.5 |   1.5 |
| Lemons |   4 |  0.25 |    1. |
#+TBLFM: $4=$2*$3

* Lesson 4: from text to a table
Data copied from elsewhere often comes as lines of text.

** 4.1 Convert comma-separated lines
={{org.table_create}}= in Visual mode turns lines of text into a table:
comma-separated (CSV) when every line has a comma, else split at tabs
or spaces. Select the three lines with =V= and =jj=, then press
={{org.table_create}}=.

city,people
Lima,10
Oslo,7

* What next
That's the spreadsheet tour. From here:

- ={{org.help}}= lists every table key; ={{org.edit_special}}= in a table
  opens its formulas in an editor, and ={{emacs.table_formula}}= asks
  for the formula of the current column.
- =:h org-tables= and =:h org-table-calc= are the manual: references,
  ranges, functions and formats.
- {{examples}} links to one file per feature, the spreadsheet included.
